EYOUTH-3101016020319-ShopSphere
Rollback Plan

Detection
- Failed releases are detected by the UptimeRobot monitoring configured in Task 1.
- UptimeRobot polls the /health endpoint every 5 minutes.
- If the endpoint returns a non-200 status or becomes unreachable, UptimeRobot sends 
  an alert and marks the monitor as DOWN.
- The on-call engineer receives the alert and confirms the failure by checking the 
  Vercel production deployment logs for build or runtime errors.

Restoration Steps
1. Log in to the Vercel Dashboard and open the ShopSphere production project.
2. Navigate to the Deployments tab.
3. Identify the previous working deployment (the one before the failed release, 
   marked as Ready and with a green check).
4. Click the three-dot menu on that deployment and select Promote to Production.
5. Wait for Vercel to complete the promotion (typically under 60 seconds).
6. Verify the rollback by opening the public application URL in a browser and 
   confirming the /health endpoint returns {"status":"ok"}.
7. Confirm UptimeRobot reports the monitor as UP again.
8. Keep the failed deployment in the history for investigation; do not delete it.
